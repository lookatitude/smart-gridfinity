# Standing design principles

## Summary

Validate by calculation, simulation and fit check before buying. Specify bought parts by specification, not brand. Keep the system modular: powered features optional, tiles join by connectors and keys, no screws between tiles. No soldering near printed parts.

## Key points

- Validate by calculation, simulation and fit check before buying.
- Specify bought parts by specification, not brand.
- Keep the system modular: powered features optional, tiles join by connectors and keys, no screws between tiles.
- No soldering near printed parts.
